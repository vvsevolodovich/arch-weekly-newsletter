## [Kubernetes upgrades don't have to break things: How EKS is making cluster lifecycle management simpler and safer](https://thenewstack.io/eks-kubernetes-upgrade-rollback/) 👨‍💼

EKS now supports in-place Kubernetes control plane rollback to the previous minor version within a seven-day window, eliminating the forced cluster recreation that previously made failed upgrades catastrophic. Pre-rollback readiness checks verify version skew compliance and add-on compatibility; etcd data, workloads, and persistent volumes survive the operation intact. EKS Auto Mode extends this by automatically sequencing worker node rollback before the control plane reverts. The feature is available at no additional cost across all commercial AWS regions. #devops #cloud #architecture

## [What's new in AI infrastructure this month](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/ai-infrastructure/whats-new-in-ai-infrastructure-this-month) 👨‍💼

Google Cloud Managed Lustre reached GA with four performance tiers — 125, 250, 500, and 1,000 MB/s per TiB — scaling to 8 PB of total storage, built on DDN EXAScaler under the hood. The managed service targets LLM training and HPC workloads in financial services, life sciences, manufacturing, and media, eliminating the operational overhead of self-managed parallel file systems at that scale. Google's August AI infrastructure roundup covers additional compute and storage updates for teams running large-scale model training on GCP. #cloud #ai #engineering
